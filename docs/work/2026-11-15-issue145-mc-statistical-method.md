## Cross-scope integration reconciliation (15 November 2026)

Independent inspection of the #146 implementation draft, [PR #164](https://github.com/Chain-Frost/python-runoff-routing/pull/164), identified two material differences from #145's original proposal.

1. The proposed `sha256-counter53-v1` deterministic, purpose-separated stream with a 128-bit seed is sufficient for versioned replay and is **now adopted** by `monte-carlo-statistics/1`, rather than needlessly requiring a separate HMAC/64-bit implementation. This does not imply native RORBwin seeded parity.
2. **Hydrological integration blocker:** #164 currently implements `linear_aep_depth_v1`; #145 specifies interpolation of gross IFD depth *linearly in the standard-normal nonexceedance deviate z*. In the specification's synthetic depths (50 mm at 50% AEP and 100 mm at 10% AEP), a 30% draw yields approximately **70.46 mm** under the adopted z-linear rule, but **75 mm** under linear-p. [Nathan–Weinmann 2013 §7.2](https://arr.ga.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0017/40562/ARR_DiscussionPaper_MonteCarlo.pdf) directly works interpolation in z; [ARR Book 4 §4.3.3.3](https://www.arr-software.org/pdfs/ARR_190514_Book4_V4.2.pdf) distinguishes normal-coordinate stratum edges from conditional rainfall draws. #146 owns a separately named z-linear interpolation implementation and source-identified test. Do not relabel old linear-p results, or adopt a different hydrological interpolation without reviewed #145 policy revision.
